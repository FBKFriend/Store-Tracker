My Sari-Sari Store

A free Android app that helps small neighborhood stores keep track of their inventory, sales and customer utang (credit). It works without internet.

Download for Android

What it does

  • Inventory with categories, sold by piece or by kilo, restocking, and low-stock alerts.
  • A point of sale with quick cash buttons, automatic change, and partial payment with the rest charged to utang.
  • Customer utang: balances, payments in installments, and history.
  • Sales history with voiding (with a reason), a dashboard, an end-of-day report, and printable PDF lists.
  • PIN lock with fingerprint unlock, and light and dark mode.

How it uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, the app uses your name and email address to show who is signed in, and it saves backups of your store data in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It cannot see or change your other Drive files, and the developer cannot access your data. Details are in the privacy policy.
