My Sari-Sari Store

My Sari-Sari Store is a free app for keeping records of a small store: inventory, sales and customer utang. It was made by Denmark Angelo C. Dela Cruz as a student project.

Privacy Policy

  • What the app stores: your items, stock, prices, sales, your customers' names and what they owe, your store name and your settings. All of it is kept in a database on your phone, so the app works without internet.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The developer does not receive, see or sell your data, and there is no account on any server of ours.
  • If you sign in with Google, the app learns your Google name and email address, only to show who is signed in. It also asks permission to keep its own private backup files in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Backups go straight from your phone to your Google Drive; the developer cannot open them.
  • If you tap "Back up locally", the app saves your data as a file in Documents/MySariSariStore on your phone. This uses Android's "All files access" permission, which the app uses only for this file. Anyone who can open your phone's files can read it, and the PIN lock does not protect it.
  • Your PIN, recovery answer and fingerprint setting stay on your phone. The PIN and recovery answer are stored scrambled (hashed) and are never part of a backup. Fingerprints are checked by Android; the app never sees them.
  • You decide what to record about your customers. Record only what you need, such as a first name and what they owe. If you run a business, you may have your own duties under privacy laws, such as the Philippines' Data Privacy Act.
  • Printing: the inventory list and shopping list PDFs are made on your phone and go only where you send them.
  • To delete your data: uninstall the app (this removes the data on your phone). To delete your Google Drive backups, open Google Drive, then Settings, Manage apps, My Sari-Sari Store, and choose "Delete hidden app data". To delete the backup file, delete the Documents/MySariSariStore folder on your phone.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new date.
